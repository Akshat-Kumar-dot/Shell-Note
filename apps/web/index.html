More than a note-taking app

ShellNote brings knowledge, applications, and AI together in a workspace designed to make working with information more connected and efficient.

  • AI-Native Data Format: A structured foundation designed to make knowledge easy for AI models and agents to read, write, and modify at a granular level with minimal token overhead.
  • Local LLMs and AI Agents: Work with local and hosted AI models and integrate intelligent agents directly into your note-taking workflow.
  • YouTube Integration: Link individual notes to specific video timestamps and revisit the exact moments behind your ideas.
  • VS Code Integration: Bring your development workflow and notes together without constantly switching between applications.
  • Connected Applications: Work with supported external applications within your ShellNote workspace.
  • MCP Integration: Connect external AI agents and tools to ShellNote through the Model Context Protocol.
